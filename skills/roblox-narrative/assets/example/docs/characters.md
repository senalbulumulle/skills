# Characters

## Mara Quell
- id: mara
- role: Keeper of the Lantern Pier lighthouse; runs the bait shop under it
- wants: the lamp lit before the storm reaches the fishing boats
- fear: that the town has stopped believing the light matters
- voice: Dry and clipped. Short sentences, sailing words, never says please. Softens only about her late brother.
- vo: Alto, late fifties, gravelly and unhurried. Deadpan humour; the warmth comes through in pauses, not pitch.

## Pip Saltby
- id: pip
- role: Dock kid who knows every crate on the pier
- wants: to be trusted with a real job
- fear: being sent home before the storm gets exciting
- voice: Fast, eager, runs sentences together, exaggerates numbers.
- vo: Bright child voice, about ten. Talks quickly and a little breathless, cracks into laughter easily.
