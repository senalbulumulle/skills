# Taste anchors: game feel

Each pair shows the stock way to "add juice" and a version tied to what the event means.

## Hit feedback

- **Generic:** every hit shakes the screen by 10 pixels and spawns 20 particles.
- **Specific:** a normal hit gets a 2-frame white flash and a 3-pixel nudge; only a killing blow gets 60 ms of hit stop and a shake, so the big reaction means something.

## Pickups

- **Generic:** coins play the same "ding" and float a "+1".
- **Specific:** each coin in a chain plays a semitone higher, and the counter scales to 1.2x and eases back over 120 ms, so a long chain sounds like a climb.

## Death

- **Generic:** the screen fades to black and "GAME OVER" appears.
- **Specific:** time slows to 20% for half a second around the hit that killed the player, the cause is labeled ("crushed by the tide gate"), and restart is one key away.
