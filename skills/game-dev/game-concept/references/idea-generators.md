# Idea generators

Use a different generator for each concept so the six ideas don't converge. Each entry has a prompt and an original example.

## 1. Verb first
Pick one verb and make it the whole game: push, pull, swap, stack, cut, bounce, rewind, merge, split, aim, hide, follow, paint, grow, trade.
- Example: *swap* — swap places with any enemy you can see; enemies only hurt what's directly in front of them.

## 2. Constraint inversion
Take a genre rule and invert it.
- Platformer, but you never jump; the level jumps.
- Shooter, but bullets are your health.
- Example: a stealth game where the guards are blind and you must stay *heard* by one guard at all times to stay safe.

## 3. System collision
Combine two unrelated systems and let one drive the other.
- Rhythm + farming, cooking + tower defense, physics stacking + dialogue.
- Example: a card game where each card you play is also a tile placed on a map your opponent walks.

## 4. Theme as metaphor
Start from a feeling or situation and find the mechanic that *is* that feeling.
- Procrastination → tasks grow if ignored. Memory loss → the map erases where you haven't looked recently.
- Example: grief — you carry objects that slow you down; setting one down at the right place opens the next area.

## 5. One resource, many uses
A single resource is ammo, health, currency, and light at once. Every spend is a trade-off.
- Example: a lantern's oil fuels your light, your shots, and your shop purchases.

## 6. Scale shift
The same rule at two scales: small actions add up to a large structure the player also interacts with.
- Example: each tiny ant you route leaves a trail; the trails become the roads a later wave of larger creatures must follow.

## 7. Limited information
Hide one thing the player normally sees: the map, the enemy, the timer, their own position.
- Example: you see only the sounds, as rings expanding from their sources.

## 8. Toy first
Build something satisfying to poke with no goal, then add the smallest goal that makes poking meaningful.
- Example: a jelly that wobbles and splits when flicked → goal: split it into exactly N pieces that land in N cups.

## Twist toolbox
When a concept feels flat, apply one:
- Time: rewind, loop, slow-motion, turn-based-only-when-you-move.
- Space: wraparound edges, gravity flips, the level rotates, two linked boards.
- Ownership: enemies become allies, your past runs become obstacles.
- Cost: every action has a visible, permanent mark on the world.

## Scope sanity (for jams)
- Pick verbs you can show with rectangles and circles.
- Prefer one screen or a simple scroll over large worlds.
- Prefer systemic depth (rules that combine) over content volume (many hand-made levels).
