# Taste anchors: game concepts

Each pair shows the generic version of a pitch and a specific one. Aim for the second: a named world, a number, and a verb the player does.

## Pitch line

- **Generic:** "An exciting roguelike where players explore dungeons, fight monsters, and collect loot."
- **Specific:** "Lanternfall: your light is your health bar. Every room you light up costs 5 seconds of flame, and monsters only exist in the dark."

## Hook

- **Generic:** "Unique gameplay mechanics keep players engaged."
- **Specific:** "Gravity flips every 8 beats of the soundtrack, so the level you memorized is upside down on the chorus."

## Title

- **Generic:** "Space Adventure", "Puzzle Quest", "Dungeon Game".
- **Specific:** "Tidelock", "Moth Post", "Cinder & Sons": a name that hints at the mechanic or the world, short enough to say out loud.
