# Roblox genre loops

Each genre below is a starting frame, not a template to copy. Add a twist from the concept step.

| Genre | Core loop | Social hook | Common pitfall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Obby (obstacle course) | run, jump, fail, retry from checkpoint | racing friends, stage counts on leaderboard | unfair jumps, long walks back after failing |
| Tycoon | buy droppers → earn → buy upgrades → expand base | visiting bases, comparing progress | idle stretches with nothing to do; pay-to-skip pressure |
| Simulator | click/collect → upgrade capacity → sell → new area | showing off pets/cosmetics, trading | numbers grow but decisions don't |
| Round-based (minigames, survival, PvP) | lobby → round → results → lobby | shared win/lose moments | waiting too long between rounds |
| Tower defense | place and upgrade towers vs waves | co-op defense | one dominant tower; slow early waves |
| Horror / escape | explore, solve, survive a threat | screaming together; split up or stick together | relies on one scare; unclear objectives |
| Roleplay / hangout | pick a role, socialize, customize | self-expression, emergent stories | no structure for new players |
| Fighting / combat | approach, attack, block, combo | duels, ranked ladders | unreadable hits; spam beats skill |
| Building / sandbox | gather, place, create | sharing creations | blank-canvas paralysis |

## Session shapes
- **Drop-in**: players join and leave anytime; progress must be personal and saved often (simulators, tycoons, obbies).
- **Round-based**: everyone shares phases; keep intermission short (about 10-20 s) and let late joiners spectate or join next round.
- **Party**: designed for friends joining together; consider private servers and team assignment by party.

## First-minute patterns
- Spawn facing the action, with the first objective visible.
- Teach with the world: arrows on the ground, glowing interactables, a short first stage that can't be failed badly.
- Give an early reward (currency, cosmetic, unlock) to establish the loop.
- Defer menus, settings, and shops until the player has played.

## Retention without pressure
- Daily rewards that accumulate rather than reset harshly.
- Weekly events or limited-time modes that bring new play, not just new prices.
- Friend incentives that reward both players for playing together.
- Visible long-term goals (an area you can see but can't reach yet).
